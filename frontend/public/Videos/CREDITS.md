# Video Footage Credits

All recorded camera feeds are real, freely-licensed US fixed-camera CCTV
footage sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org).
Clips were transcoded to H.264 MP4, resized to 640px width, and trimmed so
each impact falls late in the clip.

Caltrans D4 (CA), FDOT/FL511 (FL), NYSDOT/511NY (NY), WisDOT/511WI (WI),
